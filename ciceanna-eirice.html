⚽ Ciceanna Éirice

Cluiche Il-imreora | 🔊 Fuaim Chonnacht

1. Ina aonar: bailigh pointí. 4 soicind an focal: 5 pt laistigh de leathshoicind, ansin 4 → 1 de réir luais. Freagra mícheart = 0 pt, ach leanann tú ar aghaidh go dtí deireadh an ama.
2. Ciceanna éirice: tugann an scór is airde an chéad dúshlán. Faigheann an buaiteoir pointí uile an chaillteora, agus tá an caillteoir amuigh.
3. Buaigh agus lean ort: tugann an buaiteoir an chéad dúshlán eile.
4. Cluiche ceannais: an bheirt dheireanacha, ar na pointí uile.
Imreoir ar 0 pt tar éis an bhabhta aonair: amuigh.

🇬🇧 1. Solo: collect points. 4 seconds per word: 5 points within half a second, then 4 down to 1 by speed. A wrong answer scores 0, but you keep going until time is up.
2. Penalty shootouts: the top scorer makes the first challenge. The winner takes all the loser's points, and the loser is out.
3. Win and keep going: the winner makes the next challenge.
4. Final: the last two, for all the points.
Anyone on 0 points after the solo round is out.
